About this project

Amulets & Armor is a first-person fantasy RPG from 1997 (© United Software Artists) whose source code was later released under the GPL-3.0. This site runs that real game code, compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten. There is no DOS emulation, and nothing to install.

Your characters and settings are saved in your browser (IndexedDB). They stay on your computer, and they are lost if you clear this site's data.

This is an unofficial project. The settings let you choose a keyboard layout, and the map button in the play page toolbar adds the community quests.
